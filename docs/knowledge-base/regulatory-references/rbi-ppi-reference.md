# RBI PPI Reference (Background Only)

## 1. Purpose
This document is a background reference for understanding the general subject matter of Reserve Bank of India (RBI) frameworks related to prepaid instruments and payment products. It is not a WalletFlow policy, not a legal opinion, and not a statement that WalletFlow is authorised or covered by any RBI regime.

## 2. General context
The RBI has discussed and regulated various financial product categories, including prepaid instruments, payment services, and digital payment ecosystem frameworks. These topics are relevant to real wallet and payment businesses because they address:
- onboarding and customer due diligence
- wallet product structure and risk
- operational controls
- issuer and operatorship considerations
- compliance and reporting obligations

## 3. Why this matters to WalletFlow
WalletFlow is a fictional educational fintech application for simulation and learning. It is not a real wallet issuer, payment operator, or regulated financial institution. The existence of RBI-relevant subject matter does not by itself mean that WalletFlow is regulated or authorized.

Actual regulatory applicability depends on the real-world model, including:
- whether real money is involved
- whether funds are stored, transferred, or redeemed
- whether customer onboarding or KYC is performed
- whether the business is acting as an issuer, operator, or payment facilitator
- the legal authorisation status of the actual business
