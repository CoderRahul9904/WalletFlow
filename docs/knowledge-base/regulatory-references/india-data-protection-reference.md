# India Data Protection Reference (Background Only)

## 1. Purpose
This document provides general background on Indian data-protection and privacy-related legislation relevant to digital products that collect and process personal data. It is a reference note only and is not a legal interpretation or a statement that WalletFlow is compliant with any specific legal requirement.

## 2. Relevant subject area
In India, data protection and privacy considerations are relevant to digital platforms that handle personal and financial information. Common topics include:
- lawful processing of personal data
- minimisation of collected data
- purpose limitation and transparency
- security safeguards
- rights and governance for affected individuals
- vendor and third-party processing considerations

## 3. Why this matters to WalletFlow
WalletFlow is a fictional educational application. It is designed to avoid collecting real identity documents and to use demo-only customer information. However, any real fintech product handling actual personal or financial data would need a distinct legal review to decide whether and how applicable privacy laws apply.

## 4. Important caution
This document must not be read as:
- a legal opinion
- a compliance certification
- a representation that WalletFlow complies with privacy legislation
- a statement that the application is regulated or exempt from legal obligations

## 5. Official source references
This section is intended as a starting point for legal verification. Relevant sources may include:
- official government publications
- official legal and regulatory portals
- privacy and digital governance guidance issued by competent authorities

Any final legal conclusion must be based on current official legal texts and legal advice.

## 6. Practical application note
A real-money financial platform would need to consider data handling, retention, consent, security, cross-border transfer, and governance obligations before launch. The current WalletFlow design is intentionally limited to a demo environment and uses no real identity documents.

## 7. Mandatory note
This file is an educational reference only. It does not create legal obligations for WalletFlow or claim compliance with any data-protection framework.
