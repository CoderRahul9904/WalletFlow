# Regulatory References and Policy Distinction

## 1. Purpose
This folder contains external regulatory and legal reference materials relevant to Indian fintech context. These documents are intended for research, product background, and future assessment only. They are not internal WalletFlow policy documents and must not be treated as binding product rules.

## 2. Important distinction
WalletFlow is an educational product simulation. It is not a regulated bank, payment system operator, or prepaid instrument issuer. Internal WalletFlow policies are fictional product rules used for education and demo experiences.

The regulatory reference materials here are separate and should be interpreted as background context only. Actual legal applicability depends on:
- the real business model
- the actual services offered
- the operating jurisdiction
- the authorisations required for the business
- the final product design and risk profile

## 3. Reference scope
This folder may include notes on:
- RBI frameworks relevant to payment and wallet-like products
- India data protection and privacy frameworks
- general legal and compliance concepts relevant to fintech products

## 4. Mandatory review note
Any real-world fintech product or live wallet service must be reviewed by qualified legal and regulatory advisors before launch. This folder should not be used as a substitute for legal advice or regulatory interpretation.

## 5. Files in this folder
- rbi-ppi-reference.md
- india-data-protection-reference.md

## 6. Internal policy versus external reference
- Internal policies: WalletFlow-specific, fictional demo rules, used in product and engineering operations.
- Regulatory references: general background context, subject to review and verification.

The two are intentionally kept separate to avoid confusion between product rules and actual legal obligations.
