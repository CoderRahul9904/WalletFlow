# Customer Grievance Policy

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines the internal process for handling customer complaints and dispute-related enquiries in WalletFlow. It provides a structure for complaint intake, tracking, investigation, initial review, and resolution for the educational wallet product.

## 2. Scope and Applicability
This policy applies to:
- in-app complaint tickets
- support emails
- support investigation of wallet issues
- failed, disputed, or unclear transaction concerns
- complaints regarding wallet balances, transfer outcomes, or service experience

This policy sets internal WalletFlow service targets for the demo environment. These targets are not RBI-mandated timelines and do not represent legal obligations.

## 3. Definitions
- Complaint: A report from a user about a transaction issue, wallet discrepancy, support problem, or concern about product behavior.
- Complaint reference: A unique support identifier assigned to each complaint for tracking.
- Acknowledgement: Initial response to the complainant confirming that the issue has been received.
- Resolution: The completion of the issue review and final communication to the user.

## 4. Internal Service Targets
WalletFlow has the following fictional internal service targets:
1. Acknowledgement: 1 business day
2. Initial review: 3 business days
3. Target resolution: 7 business days

These targets are internal operating expectations for the WalletFlow demo and educational environment. They are not legal, regulatory, or RBI-mandated deadlines.

## 5. Policy Statements
### 5.1 Complaint intake
1. Customers may raise complaints through the in-app ticketing flow or support email.
2. Each complaint must receive a unique complaint reference for tracking and investigation.
3. The support team must record the complaint category, affected wallet, relevant amount, and transaction reference where available.

### 5.2 Complaint categories
Typical complaint categories include:
- failed wallet transaction
- duplicate transaction request
- wallet balance discrepancy
- transfer issue
- support delays or unclear message
- discrepancy in transaction history

### 5.3 Complaint status lifecycle
Complaint statuses should include at least:
- RECEIVED
- ACKNOWLEDGED
- UNDER_REVIEW
- RESOLVED
- ESCALATED

### 5.4 Acknowledgement
1. The support team must acknowledge receipt of the complaint within 1 business day.
2. Acknowledgement must confirm the complaint reference and indicate the next steps.

### 5.5 Initial review
1. Initial review should occur within 3 business days of acknowledgement.
2. Review should assess transaction history, wallet state, and whether the complaint relates to a failed or successful event.

### 5.6 Target resolution
1. WalletFlow aims to resolve complaints within 7 business days.
2. Resolution must be supported by transaction records and any required product or engineering review.
3. If a complaint cannot be resolved within target time, the case must remain open and the user must be updated with a revised status or reason.

## 6. Roles and Responsibilities
### Support team
- Acknowledges complaints, owns complaint references, and manages contact with the user.
- Traces wallet and transaction records to support investigation.

### Engineering team
- Reviews backend data integrity or technical issues involved in the complaint.
- Provides evidence and technical explanation when a complaint requires system analysis.

### Product team
- Reviews systemic issues and escalations affecting the product experience.

## 7. Operational Procedures
1. Customer raises a complaint through the in-app ticket or email support route.
2. Support identifies the complaint category and relevant transaction or wallet data.
3. Support acknowledges the complaint within 1 business day.
4. The complaint enters review and is investigated using wallet records and transaction history.
5. A final position is documented, and the user receives the outcome.
6. Cases that cannot be resolved within the target window are escalated and updated.

## 8. Security and Audit Considerations
1. Complaint records must be kept securely and not expose unnecessary user data.
2. Support teams must use the minimum necessary record set for dispute resolution.
3. Historical complaint records must be retained in a way that supports future review and product learning.

## 9. Exceptions and Escalation
Escalation is required when:
- multiple users are impacted by the same issue
- the complaint suggests a data integrity issue
- there is a transaction that cannot be explained by available records
- the complaint requires product policy or engineering intervention

Exceptions to the service targets may be approved for testing or educational scenarios but must be documented and communicated to the customer if needed.
