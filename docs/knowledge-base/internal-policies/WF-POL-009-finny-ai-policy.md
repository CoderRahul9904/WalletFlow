# Finny AI Policy

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines the permitted use, limitations, and responsibilities for Finny, the WalletFlow AI assistant. Finny is a read-only informational assistant intended to help users understand policies, basic financial concepts, and verified wallet activity within the educational WalletFlow application.

## 2. Scope and Applicability
This policy applies to:
- Finny assistant user interactions
- policy or general financial explanations
- wallet transaction summaries and historical review
- prompts and responses generated by AI within WalletFlow
- product, engineering, and support handling of AI-related issues

## 3. Definitions
- Finny: The WalletFlow AI assistant.
- Read-only informational assistant: An AI feature that provides education and explanation without being able to alter balances or user records.
- Verified backend data: Wallet and transaction information confirmed by the backend system records.
- Hallucination risk: The risk that an AI system provides information that is not grounded in the product’s actual data or policy source.

## 4. Policy Statements
### 4.1 Permitted activities
1. Finny may explain WalletFlow internal policies, transaction history, and general financial concepts.
2. Finny may summarise verified wallet transaction history when the relevant data exists in the backend.
3. Finny may answer user questions about product rules and educational examples.

### 4.2 Prohibited actions
1. Finny must not initiate transactions, transfer money, modify balances, or approve refunds.
2. Finny must not change user account information or wallet settings.
3. Finny must not claim that a transaction succeeded unless the backend has recorded a verified success state.
4. Finny must not invent missing product policies or create rules that are not reflected in the source documentation.
5. Finny must not present unsupported or unverified claims as fact.

### 4.3 Verified wallet data only
1. Finny must use backend-confirmed wallet and transaction data only when responding to wallet-related questions.
2. If the requested data is unavailable or uncertain, the assistant must state the limitation and ask for supporting information where appropriate.
3. Finny must not infer that a transaction succeeded based only on a user message or incomplete data.

### 4.4 Hallucination prevention and grounding
1. Responses should be grounded in internal WalletFlow policies, product rules, and verified transaction records.
2. When the answer depends on missing or ambiguous data, Finny should say it does not have enough information.
3. Finny must avoid making up legal status, regulatory claims, or compliance statements.

### 4.5 Data minimisation and privacy
1. Finny must handle wallet and user data with the minimum necessary information.
2. User prompts should not be used to expose sensitive personal information or hidden account data beyond what is necessary for the support or educational purpose.
3. If personal or sensitive content is present, the system should prefer generic or non-identifying responses.

### 4.6 Human escalation
1. Where Finny cannot answer confidently, or where a dispute, refund, or significant financial concern arises, the system should escalate to a human support flow.
2. Finny should not provide final decisions regarding disputes, chargebacks, or account corrective actions.

## 5. Roles and Responsibilities
### Product team
- Defines Finny capabilities and user-facing boundaries.
- Maintains the product policy and approved response behaviour.

### Engineering team
- Connects Finny to verified backend data and policy sources.
- Implements prompts and retrieval logic to minimise hallucination and unsupported claims.

### Support team
- Handles escalations that require human review, dispute handling, or compensation decisions.

## 6. Operational Procedures
1. Users ask Finny questions about WalletFlow policies, features, or wallet activity.
2. Finny provides explanatory or summary responses using verified data and approved policy content.
3. If a request requires action, transaction processing, or a final account decision, the assistant routes the user to a human support or product workflow.
4. If the data is insufficient or unclear, Finny clearly states the limitation instead of improvising.

## 7. Security and Audit Considerations
1. AI integrations must log relevant request context while avoiding unnecessary personal data retention.
2. The AI system must not trigger wallet mutations directly.
3. Review of AI responses should be part of operational quality checks for policy correctness and unsupported claim prevention.

## 8. Exceptions and Escalation
Any expansion of Finny beyond read-only informational assistance requires explicit approval by the WalletFlow Product & Engineering Team and a separate risk review. AI responses that affect financial decisions, refunds, or disputed account actions must be escalated to human support.

## 11. Mandatory Note
Finny is an informational AI assistant for the WalletFlow educational environment. It does not have authority to process financial transactions, approve refunds, or modify wallet balance. Actual AI governance for a real financial product requires a separate legal, operational, and compliance review.
