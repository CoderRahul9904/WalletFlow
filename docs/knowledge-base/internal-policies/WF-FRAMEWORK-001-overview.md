# WalletFlow Internal Policy Framework Overview

## 1. Purpose
This document provides the high-level structure of the WalletFlow internal policy framework. It explains the policy purpose, the educational product scope, and the distinction between internal WalletFlow rules and external regulatory references.

## 2. Scope and Applicability
This framework applies to:
- WalletFlow product and engineering teams
- support workflows
- AI-assisted user features
- internal governance and operating principles
- operational documentation used for product education and demo scenarios

## 3. Product Context
WalletFlow is a fictional educational fintech application designed to demonstrate wallet flows, transaction processing, and simple customer support scenarios. It supports:
- simulated wallet creation
- deposit, withdrawal, and transfer flows
- transaction history viewing
- user support and policy education
- Finny AI informational explanations

WalletFlow does not support:
- real-money deposits or withdrawals
- real bank account integration
- payment initiation for real funds
- actual RBI-authorised payment operations
- regulated wallet or prepaid instrument services

## 4. Internal Policy Principles
The internal policy framework is based on the following principles:
1. Demo-first, education-focused product design
2. Clear separation between fictional product rules and actual legal obligations
3. Transaction integrity and auditability
4. Data minimisation and privacy-conscious design
5. Simple but realistic support and issue handling
6. Read-only AI assistance for policy and transaction explanation
7. Explicit acknowledgement that future real-money products require separate legal review

## 5. Policy Structure
The WalletFlow policy framework includes the following internal documents:
- WF-POL-001 Wallet Operations Policy
- WF-POL-002 Transaction Management Policy
- WF-POL-003 Customer Verification Policy
- WF-POL-004 Security Policy
- WF-POL-005 Refunds and Disputes Policy
- WF-POL-006 Transaction Limits Policy
- WF-POL-007 Privacy Policy
- WF-POL-008 Customer Grievance Policy
- WF-POL-009 Finny AI Policy

These policies are intended for internal product and engineering governance and for use in RAG-based policy retrieval or educational knowledge retrieval.

## 6. Relationship to External Regulatory References
WalletFlow maintains a separate directory of external regulatory reference materials. These files explain general concepts related to:
- RBI and PPI framework context
- Indian data protection framework context
- legal and regulatory applicability assessment

These files are reference placeholders only. They are not legal policy documents and do not create obligations for WalletFlow.

## 7. Decision Summary
The core product decisions used in WalletFlow are:
- Currency: INR (₹)
- Wallet model: general-purpose simulated wallet
- Customer verification: no-KYC demo model
- Transaction limits: fixed and documented as internal product rules
- Financial actions: no real-money movement
- AI assistant: read-only informational assistant only
- Customer support: in-app and email support with internal service targets

## 8. Governance and Ownership
WalletFlow policy ownership rests with the WalletFlow Product & Engineering Team. Product and engineering changes affecting users, wallet operations, privacy, or AI functionality must be reviewed for consistency against this internal framework before release.

## 9. Operational Rules for Future Evolution
If WalletFlow evolves beyond the educational simulation into a real financial service, the following must happen before launch:
- independent legal review
- regulatory applicability assessment
- authorisation review for the specific product model
- revised customer onboarding, KYC, and privacy controls
- separate governance and compliance process
